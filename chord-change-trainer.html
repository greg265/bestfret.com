Guitar Chord Change Trainer

View on Google Play Back to Home
Practise guitar chord changes with focused, repeatable exercises designed to improve switching speed, accuracy and timing. Work on individual chord pairs, structured sequences, random changes or your own scheduled practice plan.

Practise guitar chord changes

Chord changes become easier through accurate, repeated movement. Instead of repeatedly playing an entire song, concentrate on the individual chord transitions that need work and build them into smoother, more automatic movements.

Chord change practice modes

  • Practise individual chord pairs.
  • Work through sequential chord changes.
  • Use random chord changes to test recognition and reaction.
  • Create scheduled chord-change practice.
  • Adjust exercise timing to suit your current ability.
  • Use Auto Advance for structured hands-on practice.

Improve chord transitions

Focused repetition helps develop efficient finger movement between chord shapes. Begin slowly enough to make each change accurately, then reduce hesitation as the movement becomes familiar.

Built-in practice tools

  • Guitar tuner for checking tuning before practice.
  • Metronome for practising with a steady beat.
  • Timed exercises for controlled repetition.
  • Beginner and advanced chord selections.

Who is it for?

  • Beginners learning their first guitar chords.
  • Players who hesitate when changing chords.
  • Guitarists wanting cleaner and faster chord transitions.
  • Players wanting structured guitar chord practice.
