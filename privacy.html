Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Guitar Tuner and the BestFret guitar training apps developed by Greg Harris and published under the BestFret name at BestFret.com.

Apps Covered

Microphone Access

Guitar Tuner uses the device microphone to detect musical notes and pitch for guitar tuning and simple voice pitch practice. The BestFret training apps also include a guitar tuner that uses microphone access for pitch detection.

Data Collection

BestFret apps do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Third-Party Services

These apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

User Data

No user accounts are required. No personal information is requested or stored.
